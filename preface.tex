\section*{Preface}

ok , so i want to use latex as my note taking tool for German learning, i think this is a great combination of both program and adhoc.
It's richer than just markdown, and does support custom symbols and drawings, and a lot can be reused as a module.
it's kinda like 3blue1brown visualization videos of advanced mathematic topics, i'm doing it for german learning using my own logical and critical thinking way using as much first principle method as possible (i.e. to use the least possible rules as "axioms") to form this comprehensive german learning guide.
For example, to memorize a noun in German, not only do you have to learn its meaning, but its gender and plural form, sometimes other declensions like genitive of singular masculin and neuter nouns or dativ of plural nouns.
And if you don't know much about the pattern of genders and plurals, you'll get easily overwhelmed, confused, mixed-up.
So I think before learning German and committing to memorizing vocabularies, it's important to know the basic stuff about German, understand the reason behind it.
that's the motivation of me writing this "book" in latex
